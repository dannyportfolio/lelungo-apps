# Lelungo project

Lelungo targets owners and staff of homestays, villas, guest houses, and small-to-medium hotels. It will provide a secure multi-tenant PMS while remaining operationally simple.

Current state: a working travel-deal discovery prototype plus an un-deployed tenancy/RBAC foundation. The prototype is not a PMS and must be replaced incrementally.

Near-term direction: authentication and tenant isolation verification, then small vertical PMS slices beginning with properties. Non-goals for the foundation are booking, billing, smart locks, WhatsApp, self check-in, payments, and a broad UI rewrite.
