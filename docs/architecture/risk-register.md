## Review and escalation

- Every OpenSpec proposal identifies affected risk IDs and whether likelihood, impact, mitigation, or ownership changes.
- `Critical` risks must have automated negative/concurrency evidence before the affected production workflow is enabled.
- A missing planned control is acceptable only while its triggering domain remains unimplemented; it becomes a release blocker when that domain enters scope.
- Incidents, new legal/compliance requirements, provider changes, or service extraction trigger immediate reassessment rather than waiting for a scheduled review.
